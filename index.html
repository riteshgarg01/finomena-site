    Home / About us About us

    We're building the finance back office that runs itself.

    Finance teams should spend their time on judgment, not keystrokes.

    In most companies, finance operations still run on people copying numbers from one screen to another. Invoices are keyed in by hand. Matching happens in spreadsheets. Vendors and colleagues are chased over email. Month-end becomes a marathon.

    The cost shows up everywhere: invoices that take more than a day to process, MSME vendors paid late, GST mismatches found too late to fix, and skilled people stuck doing data entry.

    Finomena exists to change that. We build AI products that do the repetitive work end to end and hand people only the decisions that need them. Large language models finally make this possible: software can now read messy documents, follow rules written in plain English, and learn an organisation's edge cases.

    What we believe

    Four principles behind every product.

    Touchless by default

    Automation should cover the whole process, not just one step of it. People step in only for real exceptions.

    Finance owns the rules

    The people who understand the process should be able to change it, in plain English, without a ticket.

    Trust is earned

    You choose how much to automate and raise it step by step. Every decision is logged and auditable.

    Built for India first

    GST, TDS, MSME rules and WhatsApp-first vendors are designed in from day one, not bolted on.
